IJAS ISEF Contest

Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair

ISEF 2027 — Los Angeles, California. Each year, the Illinois Junior Academy of Science selects nine outstanding high school researchers to represent Illinois at Regeneron ISEF, the world's largest pre-college STEM competition. Finalists are chosen through a multi-round virtual selection process involving students from IJAS regional fairs across the state.

IJAS Affiliated Fair — USIL50

IJAS Rules

  • Applicant must be enrolled in an Illinois school or Illinois-based program (such as IMSA-RISE or Illinois STEM Society) or be a homeschooled Illinois resident.
  • Applicant must be an individual IJAS member or a student of an IJAS member school.
  • Applicant must be a 9th–12th grade student.
  • Applicant and project must follow all IJAS Policy & Procedures and all ISEF rules.
  • Applicant must compete in their IJAS Regional Poster Session.
  • Submit a $20 entry fee to IJAS for the IJAS ISEF USIL50 virtual fair.

IJAS Regional Fair Information

All students participating through a regional fair must submit their abstract and required safety/endorsement forms through zFairs. zFairs opens in December.

All students wanting to participate in the IJAS State Exposition series must first compete and qualify at their Regional Fair. Students must compete in the region where they live or the region where the school or group is registered with IJAS. Membership information is under Membership.

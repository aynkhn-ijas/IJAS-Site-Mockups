President's Research Grant

Many scientifically adventurous students can be hindered by limited financial resources. The Board of Directors of the IJAS and their generous donors support students' efforts by providing grants of up to $250 per year per project.

This grant is open to all students of IJAS member schools or individual members who intend to participate in the IJAS State Exposition in Illinois. The primary focus is supplies required to conduct the experiment. Consideration is given more heavily to projects with reusable equipment, which becomes the school's property.

Application deadline: To Be Announced. Questions: info@ijas.org.

Artistic Design Contests

All 7th–12th grade students who are individual members or attend a current IJAS member school are eligible. Students do not need to compete in a science fair to enter.

T-Shirt / Program Design Contest

Theme: “Driven by Discovery: Research is Key”. Prize: $100 / $50. Deadline: TBA.

  • Line art must be dark enough to copy. Hand-drawn work should use black ink (no pencil).
  • You may use IJAS brand colors #0E3B7B, #631D33, #72A9F1, #427C54, and #75A8ED.
  • The design should not exceed 8.5"×11".
  • Original work only. No AI-created work.
  • Include “2026–2027” and the IJAS logo or a spot for the logo.
  • The designer's name should not appear on the artwork.

Decal Design Contest

2024–2025 theme: “Impossible to I'm Possible: Progress Bit by Bit”. Entry deadline March 1, 2025 (now closed).

Essay Contest

Theme: “Driven by Discovery: Research is Key”. Prizes: $100 / $50. Deadline: TBA.

Middle School (Grades 7–8): Investigate a university scientist or research team whose curiosity reshaped their field. How did their questions redefine what was possible?

High School (Grades 9–12): Build a case using real-world examples of how university research has benefitted society. How does cross-field collaboration accelerate progress?

IJAS Scholarships

Applicants must be high school seniors who attend an IJAS member school or are individual IJAS members. Students may apply for more than one scholarship but will be granted only one. Amounts are determined by the Board of Directors and/or the selection committee.
